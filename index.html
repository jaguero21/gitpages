CarpeCarb

AI-powered carb tracking

Type what you ate in plain language — “two tacos and a horchata” — and get instant carb counts with cited sources. No database searching.

  • Natural-language lookup via a Firebase proxy; API keys never touch the client
  • Two-way Apple Health sync with 30-day history
  • Siri App Intents and a Home Screen widget sharing data via App Groups

Cadence

Private daily health log

A two-minute daily check-in for people managing chronic conditions. An on-device pattern engine surfaces what's driving your symptoms, then exports a doctor-ready PDF.

  • No servers, accounts or analytics — SwiftData + your own private CloudKit
  • On-device Week Reflection with Apple Foundation Models (iOS 26)
  • iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri; English & Spanish, full VoiceOver

I spent 18 years as a construction foreman — reading plans, leading crews, and getting projects from blueprint to handover on time and on budget.

That work taught me what good software needs too: a clear plan, attention to the details nobody sees, and respect for the people who'll live with the result. I've been tinkering with Linux since 2003, and when I moved into software full-time, iOS is where it clicked.

Today I design and ship native apps end to end — architecture, UI, App Store submission, privacy policies and all. My focus is health apps that keep personal data on the device, where it belongs.

I'm open to iOS roles and freelance work. If you're building something people should be able to trust, let's talk.
